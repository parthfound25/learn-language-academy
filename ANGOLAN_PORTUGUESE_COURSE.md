# Angolan Portuguese — A1 to C2 course map

**Learn Language Academy by Parth**  
**Course scope:** 107 chapters · CEFR A1–C2 · English explanations · Angolan Portuguese focus

This course reuses the Canadian French course's 107-chapter learning path and shared lesson interface. It has a separate Portuguese content bank and separate per-language progress cache. Existing French content and progress IDs are retained.

## Standard chapter structure

1. Learning objectives
2. Vocabulary with English meanings and learner-friendly pronunciation hints
3. Grammar explanations
4. Useful expressions and examples
5. Dialogues with English translations
6. Pronunciation guidance
7. Interactive exercises with explanations
8. Speaking activities and review
9. Homework and lesson assessment

## Angolan focus

- Prioritises Angolan contexts and locally relevant vocabulary, including *candongueiro*, *machimbombo*, *funge*, *muamba* and *calulu*.
- Uses broadly understood Portuguese as a foundation while identifying Angolan words and contexts.
- Does not treat Brazilian or European Portuguese as the default Angolan variety; comparisons should be labelled when introduced.
- Pronunciation hints are learner approximations. They are not narrow IPA transcriptions and do not claim that Angola has one uniform accent.
- Learners are encouraged to listen to speakers from different Angolan regions and contexts when recordings are added.

## Development notes

This is the complete first content draft for all 107 chapters. Grammar scaffolds and assessment patterns are level-based and intentionally consistent across the path; examples, vocabulary pools and regional contexts are varied by chapter topic. A subsequent editorial pass should deepen topic-specific grammar, add lesson-specific reading/listening materials and professionally review Angolan usage. The app currently provides text-based listening guidance only; it does not yet include native audio playback or speech recognition.

Learner progress remains session-level in Streamlit. Per-language progress is separated in the active session cache, but this is not durable cross-session database persistence.
